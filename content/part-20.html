Share, apps & dashboards

Pick the right way to get content to the right people: roles, share links, apps or subscriptions.

1. Ways to distribute content

MethodBest for
Workspace rolePeople who build or edit the content (collaborators)
Share (report / dashboard link)A few people, ad hoc. Options: people in your org or specific people; Allow recipients to share; Allow recipients to build content
AppA curated, read-only bundle of reports and dashboards for a wide audience. Publish from the workspace; use audiences so groups see different content; update the app to push changes
SubscriptionAn email snapshot of a report page or dashboard on a schedule (and a link back)
Analyze in Excel / ExportPivot tables on the model; export to PDF, PowerPoint, CSV, Excel (subject to tenant settings and sensitivity labels)
Embed (Teams, SharePoint, secure embed)Show reports inside other tools with sign-in
Publish to webPublic, no authentication: never use for confidential data

Build permission on a semantic model lets people create their own reports from it (and use Analyze in Excel) without editing the model.

2. Dashboards

  • A dashboard is a single page of tiles created in the Power BI service (not in Desktop).
  • Tiles are pinned from reports (a visual or a live page), Q&A, Excel or other sources. One dashboard can combine tiles from multiple reports and semantic models.
  • Selecting a tile opens its source report. Use the Q&A box to ask questions in plain language and pin the answer.
  • Dashboards can have a mobile view and can be shared or put in an app.

3. Data alerts and subscriptions

  • Data alerts are set on dashboard tiles that show a single number: card, gauge and KPI tiles. You choose a threshold (above / below) and get a notification (optionally triggers Power Automate).
  • Subscriptions email a report page snapshot daily, weekly or after the data refresh (on capacity).
Exam trapLarge audience, read-only, different groups see different content → app with audiences. Create their own reports from your model → grant Build. Alert when a number crosses a threshold → dashboard tile alert, not a report subscription.
Key takeaways
  • Roles for collaborators, apps for audiences, share links for ad hoc access.
  • Dashboards live only in the service and combine tiles from many sources.
  • Alerts work on card, gauge and KPI tiles; “Publish to web” is public.
